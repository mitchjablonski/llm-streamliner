Empirical findings · Qwen2.5-1.5B · block-replacement + 30M-token heal

The retention number is telling you the truth about the wrong thing.

Cheap structured compression keeps ~88% of average benchmark performance at 17% compression — and quietly destroys the model's ability to reason.

One aggregate "retention %" averages a capability that is intact with one that is destroyed.

We compress a 1.5B language model with the standard cheap recipe — layer/width surgery plus a small (~30 million-token) distillation heal — and it scores well by the usual measure. Then we split the benchmark into surface/completion tasks and reasoning/knowledge tasks. The aggregate hides a crater: surface competence survives; reasoning is annihilated. The rest of this report localizes the damage to a specific axis, proves the surgery (not the heal) causes it, and shows that no cheap operator, heal, or shape brings it back.

02
Localization

Capabilities come apart along the residual width

Prune only the residual-stream width — keep all 28 layers, full attention and FFN — and sweep it. Plotted as retention vs. compression, the capabilities fan out: surface-adjacent reasoning declines gently, knowledge steeply, and multi-step math falls off a shelf.

And the earlier tempting split — "width kills math, depth kills knowledge" — is too clean. Removing depth at full width destroys math outright, and at matched parameters it does so faster than width. GSM8K is sequential arithmetic: cut the layers and the reasoning chain cannot complete, whatever the width.

Surface
Completion & commonsense tolerate heavy compression on every axis.
Knowledge
MMLU needs depth; collapses at the depth-deletion cliff (28→25 layers → chance).
Reasoning
Math needs the near-full network — width and depth — and is most fragile to depth.
03
Causal isolation

It's the cut — and nothing cheap undoes it

Four controls, each removing one explanation. Together they close every cheap escape route: it is not the heal forgetting, not the wrong basis, not the wrong heal objective, and not the wrong shape.

Reasoning is not forgotten by the heal or hidden in the wrong basis. The surgery structurally removes it, and no cheap operator, objective, or shape rebuilds what was deleted.

04
No shape escapes

Reallocating the cut doesn't help

If reasoning survived some geometry, the aspect ratio would be a lever. It isn't — though the margin does confirm depth is slightly more critical, which is exactly why native small models are built narrower-and-deeper: they protect the critical axis and re-learn narrow-width reasoning from scratch, which post-hoc compression cannot do.

05
What it means

Consequences

Report capability-stratified evals

A structured-compression result without gsm8k and MMLU is uninterpretable — the aggregate hides whether reasoning survived. This reframes existing "retention %" numbers across the literature as surface-task numbers.

Cheap reasoning-preserving compression tops out at ~4–8%

At 1.5B, by any shape. Below that, math is gone and no cheap operator or heal returns it. A 16-lever operator search and a saturated distillation sweep (loss, data, teacher, MTP, focus) confirm there is no cheap exception.

The path forward is source scale, not a better operator

Native-1.5B (width 1536, 28 layers) reasons fine, so the width and depth it occupies are sufficient. Compressing a bigger source — 3B → 1.5B — lands at an absolute geometry the 1.5B budget already proves is reasoning-capable, if the thresholds are absolute rather than relative. That is the one remaining experiment that could move the top line.
