The retention number is telling you the truth about the wrong thing.

Same model, same 17% cut. One aggregate "retention %" averages a capability that is intact (surface, 88%) with one that is destroyed (math, 6%).

We compress a 1.5B language model with the standard cheap recipe — layer/width surgery plus a small (~30 million-token) distillation heal — and it scores well by the usual measure. Then we split the benchmark into surface/completion tasks and reasoning/knowledge tasks. The aggregate hides a crater: surface competence survives while GSM8K and MMLU collapse. This case study localizes the loss across residual width and depth, tests candidate explanations (heal-induced forgetting, basis, heal objective, shape), and characterizes what the compressed models' confidence still tells you. Task-dependent collapse after layer removal was reported earlier — ShortGPT (§4.6) shows near-zero GSM8K after removing 25% of layers — so the contribution here is the cheap-heal controls, the width/depth comparison and the confidence characterization, scoped to Qwen2.5-1.5B and this ~30M-token heal.

02
Localization

Capabilities come apart along the residual width

Prune only the residual-stream width — keep all 28 layers, full attention and FFN — and sweep it. Plotted as retention vs. compression, the capabilities fan out: surface-adjacent reasoning declines gently, knowledge steeply, and multi-step math falls off a shelf.

And the earlier tempting split — "width kills math, depth kills knowledge" — is too clean. Removing depth at full width took GSM8K to zero, and at matched parameters depth removal hurt it more than width removal. One possible reading is that multi-step arithmetic depends on sequential computation across layers; these runs do not test that mechanism.

Surface
Completion & commonsense held up under heavy compression on every tested axis.
Knowledge
MMLU degraded with width and fell to chance at the depth-deletion step tested (28→25 layers).
Reasoning
GSM8K degraded under every tested width and depth cut, fastest under depth. Observed patterns for this model, not general laws.
03
Testing explanations

None of the tested cheap fixes restored it

Four controls, each testing one explanation at this ~30M-token budget: heal-induced forgetting, the choice of basis, the heal objective, and the shape of the cut.

At this budget, none of the tested reshapes, heal objectives or shapes restored baseline GSM8K. That is a statement about these controls, not proof that the capability is unrecoverable.

04
No shape escapes

Reallocating the cut doesn't help

If reasoning survived some geometry, the aspect ratio would be a lever. It isn't — though the margin does confirm depth is slightly more critical, which is exactly why native small models are built narrower-and-deeper: they protect the critical axis and re-learn narrow-width reasoning from scratch, which post-hoc compression cannot do.

And the model stops knowing what it got wrong

If a compressed model's confidence still separated right from wrong answers, hard inputs could be escalated back to the parent. On MMLU, that signal degrades with compression and is near-gone at the deeper cut:

In this setting, compression removed a capability and degraded the model's ability to signal the loss. AUROC 0.5 = confidence carries no information; the escalation threshold targets staying within 1 point of the teacher (chosen on one half, applied to the other); on the holdout the cascade ends 0.8–1.1 points below, so the target is not guaranteed. MMLU only, not a general cascade result.

06
What it means

Consequences

Report capability-stratified evals

A structured-compression result without gsm8k and MMLU is uninterpretable — the aggregate hides whether reasoning survived. An eight-task surface average alone should not be read as reasoning or knowledge retention.

Re-measure calibration after compressing

A compressed model should not inherit the parent's confidence thresholds, abstention rules or escalation policies. Confidence can degrade alongside the capability, so any confidence-based safeguard has to be re-validated on the compressed model itself.

In our tests, no shape preserved GSM8K beyond small cuts

A ~4% residual-width cut already drops GSM8K from 61.7 to 42.5; three allocations of a 21–30% cut all land at 0–1, and no tested operator or heal objective at this budget restored it. We do not claim a general maximum compression ratio: the tolerable loss depends on the application.

Open hypothesis: source scale

Native-1.5B (width 1536, 28 layers) reasons fine, so that geometry can support the capability when trained directly. Whether compressing a bigger source (3B → 1.5B) preserves more of it is an untested hypothesis; it would hold only if the thresholds are absolute rather than relative to the source.
